  • FDA granted accelerated approval to dostarlimab-gxly (Jemperli) for adult patients with untreated Stage II/III mismatch repair deficient (dMMR) or microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H) locally advanced rectal cancer.

  • In the single-arm AZUR-1 trial (n = 154), patients receiving 500 mg IV every 3 weeks for nine cycles achieved high clinical complete response (cCR) rates, enabling non-operative management without chemotherapy, radiation, or surgery.

  • FDA details Commissioner’s National Priority Voucher pilot program FDA

  • FDA announced a public hearing regarding its CNPV pilot program, designed to accelerate review times using interdisciplinary, tumor board-style evaluations.
  • Target areas include public health crisis response, novel breakthrough therapies, large unmet medical needs, and supply chain/manufacturing onshoring.

Business & Strategy

  • [Big Pharma] Merck KGaA acquires Silence Therapeutics for $1.3B to expand RNAi portfolio BioPharma Dive
  • Merck KGaA agreed to acquire Silence Therapeutics for $20.00 per U.S.-listed share in cash ($1.3 billion total value, representing a 130% premium).

  • The transaction gives Merck KGaA ownership of divesiran, an siRNA therapy with positive Phase 2 data in polycythemia vera.

  • [Big Pharma] Roche enters $15B multi-target ADC partnership with Hansoh Pharma BioPharma Dive

  • Roche will pay $400 million upfront and up to $15 billion in potential milestones to license two preclinical antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) from Hansoh Pharmaceutical and collaborate on additional targets.

  • Launch prices for new medicines show steep multi-year increase despite slight 2025 dip STAT News

  • Analysis of 202 newly launched medicines showed median net launch prices reached $257,000, up 35% from $173,000 in 2022, despite a slight decrease in the past year.

  • China proposes restrictions on foreign acquisitions of domestic biotech technology STAT News

  • Chinese regulatory authorities proposed restrictions on foreign acquisitions of critical biopharma technologies to retain domestic control over drug discovery assets.
