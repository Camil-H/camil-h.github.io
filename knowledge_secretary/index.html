Broad-spectrum chemokine inhibition prevents IL-1–mediated preterm labor in mice medRxiv - Pre-treatment with a broad-spectrum chemokine inhibitor (BSCI, 10 mg/kg IV daily) prevented intra-amniotic IL-1–induced preterm labor in pregnant C57BL/6 mice, maintaining uterine quiescence and fetal survival without altering neonatal weight. - BSCI significantly attenuated cytokine and chemokine levels in maternal plasma, myometrium, decidua, placenta, and amniotic fluid, suppressing key myometrial contraction genes (Nfkb1, Ptgs2, Akr1c18, Gja1). - Multi-omic profiling (RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, TMT-MS) showed BSCI prevented IL-1–driven chromatin accessibility shifts, extracellular matrix remodeling, and reduced uterine F4/80⁺ macrophage infiltration.

iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes in HLHS display defective mechanoadaptation to cyclic stretch bioRxiv - Patient-derived iPSC cardiomyocytes (iPSC-CMs) from Hypoplastic Left Heart Syndrome (HLHS) failed to upregulate calcium handling and sarcomeric gene expression when exposed to 20% cyclic uniaxial stretch at 1 Hz for 48 hours. - Unlike healthy controls, HLHS iPSC-CMs exhibited blunted calcium transient adaptation and mounted a pronounced inflammatory signaling response under cyclic mechanical stress.

Free testosterone correlates with muscle transcriptome but not mass or strength in aging males bioRxiv - A study of 81 healthy males aged 18–80 demonstrated that free, but not total, serum testosterone strongly correlates with vastus lateralis transcriptomic profiles. - Neither free nor total testosterone levels correlated with leg press maximum strength (e1RM) or skeletal muscle mass (measured by DEXA/pQCT), indicating testosterone's molecular active role shifts to an ancillary function in adult muscle following puberty.
