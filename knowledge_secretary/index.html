  • Plasma pTau217 demonstrates superior 11-year dementia prediction over blood NfL medRxiv

  • Longitudinal HUNT study analysis (n = 3,935; follow-up 11 years) showed plasma phosphorylated tau217 (pTau217) plus demographic factors predicted all-cause dementia with an AUC of 0.773 (95% CI: 0.756–0.790).
  • Adding multimodal risk scores yielded minor predictive gains (AUC 0.775–0.783), while adding neurofilament light chain (NfL) provided no significant improvement.

  • Stratification revealed higher predictive accuracy in APOE4-positive individuals (AUC 0.811–0.820) and a 5- to 11-fold increase in dementia odds for the highest combined risk tertile.

  • Surgical-only esophageal adenocarcinoma cohort identifies key genomic prognostic markers medRxiv

  • Genomic profiling using a 451-gene panel in 65 treatment-naive esophageal adenocarcinoma patients undergoing surgical resection (median follow-up > 11 years) established baseline prognostic mutations unconfounded by perioperative chemo-radiation.

  • Worse overall survival was significantly linked to gene amplifications in CCND1 (HR = 3.08, p = 0.02) and EGFR (HR = 2.98, p = 0.01), loss of ARID2 (HR = 2.12, p = 0.05), and missense mutations in RYR1 (HR = 2.68, p = 0.01).

  • LUCID platform leverages single-cell SRS imaging and AI for early bladder cancer detection medRxiv

  • Integrated single-cell stimulated Raman scattering (SRS) microscopy platform analyzed lipid and protein distribution across 19,678 exfoliated urinary cells from 26 bladder cancer patients and 54 non-cancer controls.

  • Automated feature extraction combined with a multiple-instance learning XGBoost model achieved non-invasive detection of bladder cancer by profiling intracellular lipid droplet dynamics.

  • GPU-accelerated RhythmWriter platform enables high-throughput cardiac safety screening bioRxiv

  • Open-source ImageJ plugin quantifies prolonged contractile motion and restitution dynamics in human iPSC-derived cardiomyocyte (hiPSC-CM) aggregates.

  • RW accurately captured rapid electrical entrainment down to 245 ms cycle lengths and S2 refractory beats down to 130 ms, enabling automated high-throughput contractility and cardiotoxicity screening.

Early Trials — Phase I–II

  • [Biotech] Imeglimin shows efficacy and safety in Phase 2 IMELD trial for MASLD with type 2 diabetes PubMed
  • Single-center, single-arm Phase 2 trial evaluated oral imeglimin hydrochloride, a novel oxidative phosphorylation fixer, in patients with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) and T2D.

  • Achieved study endpoints for hepatic safety, glycemic control, and reduction of liver fat accumulation over the treatment period.

  • High-capacity 6 mA tDCS demonstrates rapid antidepressant activity in Phase 1 pilot medRxiv

  • Open-label pilot trial evaluated accelerated high-capacity transcranial direct current stimulation (HC-tDCS) at 6 mA delivered twice daily over 5 days (20 sessions total) in 20 patients with major depressive disorder.
  • Completed with 100% session retention (203/203 sessions); mean PHQ-9 depression scores decreased from 15.8 to 7.2 (Hedges g = 1.62), with 50% achieving clinical remission at day 5.

  • Transient burning (46%), electrode-site erythema (100%), and minor residual post-inflammatory skin pigmentation at 1 month (10%, Black participants) were noted without serious adverse events.

  • Phase 2 crossover trial measures cannabidiol oil effects on auditory MMN in pediatric autism medRxiv

  • Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study (n = 19, ages 6–12) assessed 12 weeks of oral CBD oil on electrophysiological duration-deviant mismatch negativity (MMN).

  • CBD trended toward shortening peak MMN latency at the FCz electrode site relative to placebo, though the interaction did not achieve significance following false discovery rate correction (p = 0.029, pFDR = 0.058).

  • ⁶⁸Ga-Pentixafor PET/MRI localizes corticotroph adenomas in Cushing disease pilot PubMed

  • Pilot clinical imaging trial evaluated CXCR4-targeted ⁶⁸Ga-Pentixafor PET/MRI in patients with ACTH-dependent Cushing disease.
  • Non-invasive molecular imaging accurately detected microadenomas, demonstrating utility for preoperative pituitary lesion localization.

Late & Pivotal Trials — Phase III

  • [Big Pharma] Re-analysis of Phase 3 LITESPARK-022 trial shows attenuation of belzutifan benefit after treatment completion medRxiv
  • Reconstructed individual patient data from the Phase 3 LITESPARK-022 trial evaluating adjuvant belzutifan plus pembrolizumab versus pembrolizumab alone in clear cell renal cell carcinoma (RCC).
  • Hazard ratio for disease-free survival (DFS) was 0.55 (95% CI: 0.43–0.71) during the 1-year treatment phase, but shifted to 1.16 (95% CI: 0.87–1.54) after therapy stopped.

  • The 24-month restricted mean survival time difference was 1.34 months, showing early benefit attenuation similar to historical adjuvant tyrosine kinase inhibitor trials.

  • [Big Pharma] Phase 3 exit interviews characterize patient preference for oral GLP-1 orforglipron over oral semaglutide PubMed

  • Qualitative patient exit interviews conducted alongside a Phase 3 head-to-head trial comparing Eli Lilly's once-daily non-peptide oral GLP-1 agonist orforglipron to Novo Nordisk's oral semaglutide in type 2 diabetes.

  • Participants reported improved satisfaction regarding administration flexibility and daily dosing logistics without rigid food and water fasting restrictions.

  • [Big Pharma] Post hoc Phase 3 analysis quantifies brexpiprazole effects on agitation domains in Alzheimer's dementia PubMed

  • Post hoc analysis of pivotal Phase 3 trials evaluated caregiver-reported outcomes for brexpiprazole (Rexulti) across specific behavioral subdomains in Alzheimer's dementia.
  • Treatment yielded consistent, domain-specific reductions in aggressive behavior, verbal agitation, and physical restlessness compared to placebo.
