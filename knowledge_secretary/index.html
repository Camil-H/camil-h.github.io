  • CSF PrP-amyloid seeding predicts symptomatic conversion in inherited prion disease — bioRxiv

  • Longitudinal evaluation of 94 PRNP E200K mutation carriers demonstrated that real-time quaking-induced conversion (RT-QuIC) positivity in cerebrospinal fluid occurs a median of 2.0 years (IQR 1.3–3.3) before clinical symptom onset.

  • Plasma GFAP mediates amyloid-tau progression across the Alzheimer's spectrum — bioRxiv

  • Integrated fluid and PET biomarker analyses across two independent cohorts (n = 278 and n = 236) show plasma glial fibrillary acidic protein (GFAP) statistically mediates the relationship between soluble Aβ₄₂/₄₀ ratios and amyloid PET, as well as between amyloid PET and tau PET accumulation.
