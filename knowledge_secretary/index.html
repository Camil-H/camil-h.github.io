Discovery & Translational Science

  • Single-cell analysis reveals mechanism of resistance to dual checkpoint blockade in mCRC bioRxiv
  • Single-cell transcriptomic and T-cell receptor (TCR) profiling of paired biopsies from the Phase II SERPENTINE trial of dual PD-L1 and CTLA-4 blockade in mismatch repair-proficient (pMMR/MSS) metastatic colorectal cancer showed that clinical benefit was restricted to patients without liver metastases.

  • While dual checkpoint inhibition successfully induced systemic T-cell expansion across all patients, the local hepatic microenvironment prevented therapy-expanded T-cell clones from establishing tissue-resident memory phenotypes in liver lesions.

  • Sodium oligomannate targets gut-brain axis to elevate protective metabolite in Alzheimer's model PubMed

  • Preclinical research shows sodium oligomannate remodels gut redox adaptation, driving gut microbiota to produce elevated levels of indole-3-propionic acid (IPA).

  • The elevated IPA production contributed directly to the mitigation of neuroinflammation and Alzheimer's disease pathology in vivo.

  • Vascular injury study demonstrates spatial uncoupling of endothelial Ca²⁺ signaling from procoagulant membrane scrambling bioRxiv

  • Intravital imaging of vascular injury models revealed that focal injury triggers a rapid intercellular Ca²⁺ wave extending ~200 μm (engaging ~50 cells) via extracellular ATP diffusion and IP₃-dependent Ca²⁺ release.

  • Despite widespread Ca²⁺ elevation, phosphatidylserine (PS) externalization and procoagulant membrane scrambling remained confined exclusively to cells directly adjacent to the wound edge, preventing intravascular thrombosis during injury signaling.

  • Post-pubertal testicular factor exposure drives elevated male mortality bioRxiv

  • Castration of male UM-HET3 mice at pre-pubertal (20 days), post-pubertal (60 days), or late-life (240 days) stages extended median lifespan by 19.3%, 25.8%, and 32.9%, respectively.

  • Results demonstrate that the male mortality penalty is maintained by active post-pubertal testicular factor exposure rather than a permanent imprint established during pubertal development.

  • Ataraxis Breast CTX AI biomarker externally validated on Phase III SWOG-8814 trial cohort bioRxiv

  • A blinded external evaluation of Ataraxis Breast CTX (a causal multimodal AI model integrating H&E digital histology and clinical features) was conducted on 677 postmenopausal, node-positive, HR+ breast cancer patients from the Phase III SWOG-8814 trial.
  • Both histology-only and multimodal AI scores significantly predicted disease-free survival without requiring tissue-consuming genomic assays.

Elevenostat demonstrated to be off-target for HDAC11 Science.org * Re-evaluation of elevenostat, long utilized as an HDAC11-selective probe, demonstrates only weak binding to HDAC11 (~10 μM). * The molecule exhibits single-digit nanomolar affinity for HDAC6 and HDAC10, indicating its cellular activity is mediated primarily through these off-target deacetylases.

Multi-omic profiling links plasma AD biomarkers to disease progression modules medRxiv * A study in 484 adults mapped clinical blood biomarkers (Aβ42/40, p-tau181, p-tau217, GFAP, NfL) across >6,000 plasma proteins. * Linear modeling defined specific, overlapping proteomic signatures and cellular pathways tracking systemic pathology across cognitive stages.

Renal GDF15 acts as a required regulator against hypokalemia in primary aldosteronism bioRxiv * Patients with primary aldosteronism displayed a 3-fold elevation in urinary GDF15 and a 1.6-fold increase in plasma GDF15, which normalized post-treatment. * Murine mineralocorticoid excess models confirmed that GDF15 drives type A intercalated cell proliferation and collecting duct K⁺ reabsorption to prevent severe hypokalemia.
