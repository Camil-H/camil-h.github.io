Causal network mapping of sEEG contacts identifies epileptogenic targets in drug-resistant epilepsy medRxiv - Evaluated a quantitative metric termed "criticality" using causal network mapping and machine learning across 97 patients undergoing stereoelectroencephalography (sEEG) for drug-resistant epilepsy. - Contacts with high criticality scores strongly aligned with surgically resected brain regions in patients who subsequently achieved seizure freedom. - The criticality algorithm outperformed neural fragility models by accounting for time-delayed connections and cross-frequency interactions.
