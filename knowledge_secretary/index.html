State of AI antibody design highlights target selection and immunogenicity hurdles — Science - Computational antibody design benefits from favorable baseline pharmacological properties, such as long plasma half-lives, but key translational bottlenecks persist. - Unintended target biology (e.g., recent clinical anti-IL-6 failures in cardiovascular settings), off-target cross-reactivity, and anti-drug antibody immunogenicity remain critical failure modes for AI-generated candidates.

BAP1/ASXL3 complex regulates α-synuclein uptake via glycoproteome remodeling — bioRxiv - A genome-wide CRISPR activation screen identified BRCA1-associated protein 1 (BAP1) as a key genetic modifier of α-synuclein fibril internalization. - BAP1 acts as a master transcriptional regulator of the cell surface glycoproteome, downregulating heparan sulfate proteoglycans while upregulating O-linked glycoproteins to reduce fibril entry. - Overexpression reduced α-synuclein uptake in iPSC-derived human dopaminergic neurons without impacting microglial phagocytosis.

Macrophage VEGFA signaling maintains aqueous humor outflow in aged eyes — bioRxiv - Single-cell sequencing and tissue imaging revealed age-dependent accumulation of peri-Schlemm’s canal (SC) macrophages alongside elevated VEGFA-VEGFR signaling in ocular angle tissues. - Conditional deletion of Vegfa in CX3CR1⁺ macrophages led to elevated intraocular pressure and reduced aqueous humor outflow facility in 9-month-old mice. - Findings indicate that macrophage-derived VEGFA acts as a homeostatic compensatory mechanism against age-related trabecular meshwork degradation.

Mitochondrial STARD7 inhibition blocks influenza virus replication — PubMed - Small-molecule screen identified targeting of the mitochondrial lipid transfer protein STARD7 as a therapeutic strategy against seasonal influenza. - Chemical inhibition of STARD7 disrupted viral replication dynamics, demonstrating the role of mitochondrial lipid transport machinery in supporting viral life cycles.

MGAT1 identified as a synthetic lethal target in STK11-mutant lung cancer — PubMed - Combined genetic and biochemical screening identified mannosyl (α-1,3-)-glycoprotein β-1,2-N-acetylglucosaminyltransferase (MGAT1) as a druggable vulnerability in STK11-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). - Inhibition of MGAT1 disrupted aberrant N-glycosylation pathways required for cell survival in STK11-deficient metabolic states.

Plasma p-tau217 tracks amyloid-independent neurodegeneration in Tuberous Sclerosis Complex — medRxiv - Comparative biomarker evaluation across 63 patients with Tuberous Sclerosis Complex (TSC) revealed significant elevations in plasma phosphorylated tau-217 (p-tau217) relative to unimpaired controls. - Hyperactivation of the mTOR pathway driven by TSC1/2 loss of function promotes tau hyperphosphorylation independent of amyloid-β deposition, supporting p-tau217 as a pharmacodynamic biomarker for mTOR-targeted therapies.

In vivo CD19 cell therapy landscape map details target and platform strategies — X / @andrewpannu - An industry mapping of more than 90 in vivo CD19 cell therapy programs across 71 entities highlights that CD19 + BCMA remains the primary multi-target strategy, though single-target CD19 strategies continue to provide a distinct B-cell reset profile with potential safety advantages. - Adding BCMA targets long-lived plasma cells capable of escaping CD19-only depletion, offering potential therapeutic utility across both oncology and autoimmune disease indications. - Delivery platforms—including linear mRNA/tLNPs, circular RNA/tLNPs, and targeted lentiviral vectors—exert major influence over CAR expression duration and suitability for repeat dosing.
