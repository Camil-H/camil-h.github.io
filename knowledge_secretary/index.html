  • [Biotech] [Functional phenotyping of ADPKD cysts via 23Na MRI] — Preprint
  • Researchers developed a non-invasive $1.8\text{-fold}$ more frequent 7-Tesla sodium ($^{23}\text{Na}$) magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) method to phenotype autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD) cysts in vivo.
  • The imaging revealed two distinct cyst types: high-$[\text{Na}^+]$ cysts, which express the proximal tubular marker SGLT2, and low-$[\text{Na}^+]$ cysts, which express distal tubule/collecting duct markers and exclusively express the vasopressin V2 receptor (V2R)—the therapeutic target of tolvaptan.

  • Marked interindividual variation in cyst composition was observed, with low-$[\text{Na}^+]$ cysts comprising between 4.6% and 83.9% of all cysts, offering a potential functional biomarker to stratify patient response to V2R antagonists.

  • [Digital biomarkers for Multiple Sclerosis motor-cognitive planning] — Preprint

  • Luria's fist-edge-palm paradigm was digitized into a self-administered smartphone task within the Neurological Functional Test Suite (NeuFun-TS) to evaluate 296 participants (including 208 people with MS).
  • A parsimonious two-biomarker composite differentiated disease severity (healthy donors < relapsing-remitting MS < progressive MS), demonstrating strong correlation with MRI-measured CNS tissue destruction ($\rho = 0.29\text{–}0.38$) and clinician disability scales ($\rho = 0.44\text{–}0.52$).

  • Kinematic decomposition successfully dissociated cognitive planning latency from pure motor execution speed during the sequencing tasks.
