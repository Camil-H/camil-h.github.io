  • Mechanistic considerations for targeted protein degradation vs. inhibition Science
  • Targeted protein degradation (TPD) offers a distinct pharmacological profile from occupancy-based small-molecule inhibitors by physically removing target proteins via cytosolic proteasomal pathways.

  • Efficacy and selectivity of TPD agents are highly contingent on target-specific baseline synthesis rates, intrinsic half-life, physiological clearance pathways, and target overexpression patterns in disease tissues.

  • Serum brain-derived tau differentiates acute TBI from spinal cord injury medRxiv

  • Adult body weight modifies lifespan extension efficacy in mouse models bioRxiv
