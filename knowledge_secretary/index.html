  • Targeting TMPRSS6 in metabolic liver disease raises cardiovascular and lipid safety concerns medRxiv
  • Mendelian randomization analyzing genetic TMPRSS6 (matriptase-2) inhibition demonstrated lower liver iron content (β = -2.50, p < 0.001) but increased corrected T1 (cT1, β = 1.59, p < 0.001), indicating potential inflammatory or fibrotic exacerbation.

  • Biomarker research links parietal periodic gamma EEG activity to amyloid and tau pathology medRxiv

Discovery & Translational Science

  • Biological age uncertainty identified as novel healthspan predictor bioRxiv
  • A probabilistic framework applied to over 450,000 UK Biobank participants across NMR metabolomic, plasma proteomic, and blood test profiles established Biological Age Uncertainty (BAU) from the width of predicted-age distributions.
  • Higher BAU independently predicted mortality (hazard ratios of 1.24 for NMR and 1.40 for proteomic BAU per standard deviation) and multimorbidity beyond standard biological age gap metrics.

  • Genetic and physiological analyses linked elevated BAU to multi-organ discordant aging, chronic inflammation, metabolic dysregulation, and tissue remodeling.

  • Repetitive negative thinking linked to longitudinal amyloid accumulation medRxiv

  • Longitudinal neuroimaging in 94 cognitively healthy adults demonstrated that repetitive negative thinking (RNT) significantly predicted increased global cortical amyloid deposition over 3 years (p = 0.012, Florbetapir-PET).
  • Voxelwise analysis localized amyloid accumulation to early Alzheimer's disease-vulnerable regions, including the precuneus, posterior cingulate cortex, and medial frontal cortex.

  • RNT associations remained significant after controlling for anxiety and depressive symptoms, while no changes were observed in FDG-PET glucose metabolism or volumetric brain atrophy.

  • Burn wound excision drives acute hypermetabolic response in preclinical models bioRxiv

  • A controlled mouse model evaluating ~15% body surface area scald burns revealed that surgical wound excision increased resting energy expenditure (REE) by 102%–136% during the first week post-injury compared to controls.
  • Excision (alone or combined with scald burn) resulted in significantly greater acute body mass loss, cachexia, and bone mineral density reduction than burn injury alone.

  • Results indicate that rodent models must incorporate burn wound excision to accurately model clinical hypermetabolism and cachectic responses.

  • Epigenetic methylation score tracks lifetime depression exposure medRxiv

  • Evaluation of a 15-site blood DNA methylation score (MD-MS) in 731 NESDA cohort participants showed significantly higher MD-MS levels in individuals with lifetime major depressive disorder (p = 0.012) and chronic depression trajectories over 6 years (p = 0.001).
  • MD-MS positively correlated with inflammatory (C-reactive protein) and metabolic biomarkers (triglycerides, fasting glucose).

  • Adjustment for psychosocial variables (childhood trauma, social activity) attenuated the association between MD-MS and lifetime depression by 17.5%.

  • Slide-level attention CNNs benchmarked for serous ovarian carcinoma grading medRxiv

  • A leakage-controlled evaluation on 259 whole-slide images (UBC-OCEAN cohort) demonstrated that an ImageNet-pretrained MobileNet with differential attention achieved an AUC of 0.862 in differentiating high-grade from low-grade serous carcinoma.
  • The pretrained MobileNet significantly outperformed no-attention baselines and the Phikon pathology foundation model benchmark (AUC 0.834).

  • Adding spatial attention branches to channel attention produced no detectable performance gain (AUC 0.824 to 0.822), highlighting the critical role of transfer learning over spatial module complexity.

  • Machine learning detects chronic kidney disease from routine full blood counts medRxiv

  • Evaluation of 155 patient records from a Ghanaian hospital demonstrated that ML models using 8 raw full blood count (FBC) parameters plus age detected CKD with 83.9% accuracy and an AUC of 0.939 when synthetic oversampling (SMOTE) was applied prior to splitting.

  • Restricting resampling to training folds yielded a held-out testing accuracy of 61.5% and an AUC of 0.604 across 39 unseen patients, highlighting a 22.4 percentage-point performance shift based on preprocessing design.

  • Rapid 6-hour direct-from-urine assay triages suspected urinary tract infections medRxiv

  • A prospective diagnostic accuracy study (n=120) evaluating SIBURI/SIBURI+ (nitrate-reduction and bromothymol blue broths) demonstrated 92.7% sensitivity, 92.4% specificity, and a 96.1% negative predictive value against quantitative urine culture.

  • SIBURI alone identified 70.7% of culture-positive specimens, while SIBURI+ detected non-nitrate-reducing pathogens including enterococci, streptococci, Acinetobacter, and Candida.
