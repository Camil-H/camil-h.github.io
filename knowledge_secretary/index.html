Business & Strategy

  • [Big Pharma] Halozyme wins European patent injunction halting Merck's subcutaneous Keytruda STAT+ / BioPharma Dive
  • A Netherlands court has ordered a halt to the manufacturing and marketing of Merck’s subcutaneous formulation of pembrolizumab (Keytruda Qlex) in eight European countries.
  • The court ruled that Merck’s subcutaneous formulation infringed on Halozyme's patents covering technology designed to deliver large biologics via under-the-skin injection.

  • Halozyme has also won a similar legal proceeding in Germany and is actively challenging Merck in U.S. court. Competitors Bristol Myers Squibb and Roche have licensed Halozyme's technology for their respective subcutaneous products (Opdivo and Tecentriq).
