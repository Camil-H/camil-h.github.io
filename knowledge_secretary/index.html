  • Experimental studies in human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived ventricular cardiomyocytes (hiPSC-CMs) and rat models showed purified ciguatoxins (CTX-1B and CTX-3C) induce rapid diastolic intracellular [Ca²⁺] alterations and RyR2-dependent calcium leaks.

  • Transcranial photobiomodulation restores cortical alpha power and working memory in ADHD medRxiv

  • A double-blind, sham-controlled crossover trial evaluated 16-minute sessions of 150 mW right prefrontal transcranial photobiomodulation (tPBM) in 28 adults with ADHD versus 29 healthy controls.
