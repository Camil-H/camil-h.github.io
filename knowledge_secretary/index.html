  • Circulating tumor DNA detects subclinical cancer in systemic sclerosis and dermatomyositis medRxiv

  • Prospective tracking of 134 adults with recent-onset systemic sclerosis (SSc) or dermatomyositis (DM) identified circulating tumor DNA (ctDNA) in 4 of 6 patients who subsequently developed clinically diagnosed malignancies.

  • Plasma ctDNA positivity preceded clinical cancer detection by a median of 13 months (range 1.4–17), showing identical somatic mutations between cell-free DNA and matched primary tumor tissue.

  • Serum IGF-1 levels correlate with attenuated functional decline in ALS medRxiv

  • Longitudinal evaluation across 48 inter-visit intervals in 37 amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) patients revealed that each 1-SD higher serum IGF-1 level at a prior visit predicted a 1.40-point lesser subsequent decline on the ALSFRS-R functional scale.

  • Findings suggest systemic IGF-1 serves as a circulating trophic-metabolic marker of functional reserve in ALS.

  • Single-cell spatial transcriptomics identifies healthy donor kidney aging signature reflected in diabetic kidney disease medRxiv

  • Profiling 24 healthy living donor biopsies (aged 36–66) using a 5,101-gene spatial transcriptomics panel revealed age-dependent loss of cellular contact between immune cells and tubular epithelium.

  • Applying the living-donor tissue-aging score to 28 diabetic kidney disease (DKD) biopsies independently predicted post-biopsy eGFR decline, with aging scores successfully predicted directly from routine periodic acid-Schiff (PAS) histology slides.

  • Clonal hematopoiesis linked to elevated risk of lung adenocarcinoma and esophageal carcinoma medRxiv

  • Prospective UK Biobank cohort analysis linked clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential (CHIP) to incident solid tumors overall (HR 1.13, 95% CI: 1.08–1.19).

  • Histologic subtype analysis revealed statistically significant associations specifically for lung adenocarcinoma (HR 1.79) and esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (HR 2.28), with risk escalating alongside clone size and multi-hit mutations.

  • Histone deacetylase inhibitor Trichostatin A rescues disuse muscle atrophy bioRxiv

  • In a 14-day murine hindlimb suspension model, systemic administration of Trichostatin A (TSA) restored disrupted Lamin A/C organization and H3K9me3 heterochromatin foci.

  • HDAC inhibition significantly attenuated the upregulation of atrophy-related gene expression networks and prevented loss of locomotor activity.

  • Satellite cell glucocorticoid receptor restrains myogenic commitment bioRxiv

  • Conditional deletion of the glucocorticoid receptor (GR) in satellite cells reduced PAX7⁺ stem cell counts during muscle regeneration in young mice without compromising net tissue repair.
  • GR silencing in human myoblasts induced early myogenic differentiation markers (including MYOG) without altering proliferation, demonstrating a conserved role for GR in maintaining stem cell quiescence.
