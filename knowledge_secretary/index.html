Discovery & Translational Science

  • Anti-seizure drug levetiracetam targets diffuse midline glioma — Nature Medicine
  • Levetiracetam selectively dampens GABAergic synaptic signaling, leading to reduced tumor growth and prolonged survival in experimental models and patient cohorts of diffuse midline glioma.

  • This therapeutic effect is specific to midline gliomas (not hemispheric high-grade gliomas) and operates independently of the drug's canonical SV2A-mediated primary anti-seizure mechanism.

  • Manganese availability regulates Akt activity and insulin sensitivity — bioRxiv/medRxiv

  • Hepatocyte manganese (Mn) concentrations act as a key determinant of PI3K-independent Akt function in vivo, with Mn levels fluctuating during fasting and feeding via the transporter SLC30A10.

  • Mn increases Akt's catalytic efficiency, acting additively with insulin to enhance Akt activity and suppress hepatic glucose output.

  • Scn9a inhibition protects pancreatic beta cells in type 1 diabetes — bioRxiv/medRxiv

  • Pharmacological or genetic knockout of the Na⁺ channel β-subunit gene Scn9a (Nav1.7) in NOD mice significantly reduced β-cell Na⁺ currents, Ca²⁺ responses, and insulin secretion under high-glucose conditions.

  • The sodium channel inhibitor carbamazepine protects human and mouse β-cell survival in vitro and in vivo, and its protective pathway is mediated through Scn9a.

  • Large-scale oncology pharmacogenomics platform maps systemic toxicity determinants — bioRxiv/medRxiv

  • Researchers developed MSK-Tox, an LLM-based pan-cancer resource capturing clinical toxicity events (pneumonitis, liver toxicity, colitis, etc.) across more than 50,000 patients.

  • The analysis identified a germline regulatory variant near FOXE1 that confers a high risk of thyroid toxicity across multiple systemic therapy regimens.

  • Genetic variant drives 25-fold higher lung cancer risk in never-smokers — STAT News

  • A newly identified, highly rare genetic variant has been associated with a 25-fold increase in the odds of developing lung cancer among individuals who have never smoked.

  • [Biotech] Laromestrocel stem cell therapy preserves brain volume in mild Alzheimer's — bioRxiv/medRxiv

  • A Phase I/II trial (N=49) of the mesenchymal stem cell therapy laromestrocel in mild Alzheimer's disease demonstrated significant inhibition of progressive brain inflammation.
  • Compared to placebo (N=9), subjects receiving monthly doses of 25 million (25M) or 100 million (100M) cells over 39 weeks showed reduced neuroinflammation in the left hippocampus (p < 0.001) and reduced plasma neurogranin (p = 0.023).

  • Decreased neuroinflammation correlated directly with reduced brain atrophy (R = -0.326, p = 0.040) and improved MoCA cognitive scores (R = -0.401, p = 0.011).

  • Closed-loop auditory stimulation during sleep improves memory in early Alzheimer's — bioRxiv/medRxiv

  • A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover trial (N=37) in patients with biomarker-confirmed early symptomatic Alzheimer's tested one night of closed-loop auditory stimulation (CLAS) of slow sleep oscillations.

  • CLAS significantly improved overnight declarative memory retention (word-pair consolidation, Cohen's dz = 0.42, p = 0.04) and enhanced slow-wave and fast spindle electrophysiological activity.

  • [Big Pharma] Compounded microdose tirzepatide achieves clinical weight loss — bioRxiv/medRxiv

  • A retrospective observational study analyzed 93 clinical measurements from obese adults (BMI ≥27) initiating compounded tirzepatide at microdoses of 1 mg or 2 mg weekly—substantially lower than the standard 2.5 mg/week starting dose.

  • Patients demonstrated significant weight reductions within 14–89 days of treatment exposure before undergoing any dose escalation.

Late & Pivotal Trials — Phase III

  • [Big Pharma] Pembrolizumab combination extends survival in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer — The Lancet
  • Data from the Phase III ENGOT-ov65/KEYNOTE-B96 trial show that pembrolizumab plus weekly paclitaxel, with or without bevacizumab, is highly active in patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer who received ≤2 prior lines of therapy.

  • The regimen demonstrated clinically significant improvements in progression-free survival (PFS) and clinically meaningful gains in overall survival (OS) in a historically poor-prognosis setting.

  • GLP-1 receptor agonist initiation is associated with 60% excess muscle loss — bioRxiv/medRxiv

  • A prospective, population-based MRI study matched 58 adults with diabetes initiating GLP-1 RAs against 130 unexposed controls over 4.6 years.
  • GLP-1 RA initiators experienced 2.75 percentage points more skeletal muscle volume loss (95% CI -4.51 to -0.99; p = 0.003) and 2.54 percentage points greater weight loss (p = 0.021) than controls.

  • Skeletal muscle volume decreased by roughly 60% more in the GLP-1 RA cohort, while differences in adipose tissue reductions (internal torso and subcutaneous) were statistically non-significant.

[Biotech] FDA expands approval of Pharming's Joenja to pediatric APDS patients — FDA - FDA granted label expansion for Joenja (leniolisib), a selective PI3Kδ inhibitor, extending its indication to pediatric patients aged 4 to 11 years (weighing ≥27 kg) with activated phosphoinositide 3-kinase delta syndrome (APDS). - Weight-based oral dosing (40 mg, 50 mg, or 70 mg twice daily) was approved to target persistent lymphoproliferation and primary immunodeficiency in younger cohorts.

FDA announces public hearing on clinical framework for therapeutic psychedelics — FDA - The agency scheduled a public hearing for September 14, 2026, to discuss regulatory pathways, clinical trial designs, and safety monitoring requirements for therapeutic psychedelic candidates. - Sessions will address standardized delivery, psychotherapy integration, and risk mitigation strategies in supervised clinical environments.

US CDC concludes largest recorded Cyclospora outbreak — STAT+ - Public health agencies officially closed the investigation into the largest domestic outbreak of Cyclospora cayetanensis food poisoning in US history. - The specific agricultural vector or supply chain point of origin remains unidentified despite extensive traceback analyses.

Hierarchical Bayesian analysis uncovers sub-national transmission dynamics in DRC Bundibugyo outbreak — medRxiv - Spatial modeling of the 2026 Bundibugyo virus disease (BDBV) outbreak in the Democratic Republic of the Congo revealed marked divergent trajectories obscured by national reproduction number (Rt) averages. - Epicenter regions (Ituri) showed sustained decline (median Rt < 1.0), whereas newly seeded provincial fronts maintained active expansion vectors, underscoring the necessity of spatially granular public health interventions.
